Tesla Flying Car

Tesla's new Roadster has been shown with a special flying capability using advanced thruster technology. It can perform short airborne movements and impressive aerial stunts. Tesla says the Rocket version is designed with a full flight system, although it is not approved for public road use. The technology could represent an exciting step toward the future of flying cars.
